About this data

Purchases and sales are sourced through CongressInvests from public House and Senate filings. Disclosures can arrive up to 45 days after a transaction. Directional return runs from the first completed daily close after the disclosure became public, the earliest price a reader could act on. Disclosures give value ranges rather than execution prices. Informational only, not financial advice.

About this data

Insider filings are sourced through API Ninjas. Coverage is limited to the latest 10 filings per check, so busy periods may have gaps. It is not a realized return or a recommendation.
