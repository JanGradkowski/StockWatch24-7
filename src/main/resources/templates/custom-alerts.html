How custom alerts work

  1. Describe the moment you care about

    Add conditions such as “RSI below 30”.

  2. Choose when it fires

    Usually when the whole rule becomes true.

  3. Point it at your stocks

    Apply it to a whole watchlist or to chosen stocks.

  4. Read why it fired

    Each trigger lists every condition with the value that met it.

Alerts read completed candles only. Informational only, not financial advice.
