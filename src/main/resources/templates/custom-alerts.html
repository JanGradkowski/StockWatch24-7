How custom alerts workFour steps, about a minute to read
  1. Describe the moment you care about

    Add conditions such as “RSI below 30”, “a bullish Elliott Wave II end in the last 5 candles” or “insiders bought in the last 30 days”. Join them with All (every condition must be true) or Any (one is enough).

  2. Choose when it fires

    Usually when the whole rule becomes true. It then stays quiet while the rule stays true and re-arms once it is false again. You can also fire once per stock or on every candle.

  3. Point it at your stocks

    Apply it to a whole watchlist (stocks you add later are included) or to chosen stocks. The same alert can watch several lists.

  4. Read why it fired

    Each trigger lists every condition with the value that met it, and keeps measuring how the price moved afterwards. Click an alert to see all its triggers.

Alerts read completed candles only, so a candle that is still forming can never trigger them. Informational only, not financial advice.
