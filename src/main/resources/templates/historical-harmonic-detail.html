How the pattern is scored

A harmonic signal is rejected when any mandatory direction, pivot-order, or point-boundary rule fails. Valid formations begin at 100, and only tolerated deviations from non-hard Fibonacci targets, leg equality, and preferred completion ratios reduce the score. It is not a probability of profit or financial advice.
