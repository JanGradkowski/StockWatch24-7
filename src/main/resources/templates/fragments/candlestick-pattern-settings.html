01

Candlestick pattern definitions

Default settings

Control the completed-candle measurements that define each formation and the close-based trade plan opened after detection. These settings apply to future alerts and newly generated historical scans.

How values are measured Percent (%) compares a real body with the candle's complete high-to-low range. Multiple (×) compares a shadow or body with another body or the recent median body.

The separate Detection settings control how StockWatch identifies an uptrend or downtrend. Existing stored signals retain their original stop, target, and ratio snapshot.

02

Risk-to-reward

Default trade plan

Set one reward multiple for all intervals. A value of 2 means the target is twice as far from entry as the configured stop loss. Once you change it, the same multiple is also the minimum for Elliott Wave and harmonic trade plans (their defaults are 1:2 daily and 1:3 weekly or monthly). Trades enter at the open of the candle after the signal and close at the end of the saved horizon if neither level is reached.

Structural risk checks

New plans apply the stop exactly as configured: the structural price moved out by your buffer percent, or a fixed percent from entry. A distant stop or insufficient reward to the nearest target makes the setup projection only. Stops are never pulled inside the structure to manufacture a reward/risk ratio.

Daily limits: 3 ATR / 12.5%. Weekly: 4 ATR / 20%. Monthly: 5 ATR / 30%. These are initial safeguards, not estimated success probabilities. Daily and weekly candlestick horizons are 8 bars; monthly is 6.
