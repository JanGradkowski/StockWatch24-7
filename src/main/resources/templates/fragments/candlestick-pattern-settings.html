Candlestick pattern definitions

Factory definitions

Control the completed-candle measurements that define each formation and the close-based trade plan opened after detection. These settings apply to future alerts and newly generated historical scans.

How values are measured Percent (%) compares a real body with the candle's complete high-to-low range. Multiple (×) compares a shadow or body with another body or the recent median body.

The separate Detection settings control how StockWatch identifies an uptrend or downtrend. Existing stored signals retain their original stop, target, and ratio snapshot.

02

Risk-to-reward by interval

Factory trade plan

Set the reward multiple independently for each interval. A value of 2 means the target is twice as far from entry as the configured stop loss. Every trade still expires at candle 8 if neither close boundary is reached.

03

ATR target circuit breaker

Factory safeguards

When the normal structural/custom stop would produce a target move above the selected percentage, replace it with a frozen ATR-based risk distance. If that ATR distance would still exceed the limit, the percentage cap determines the final risk. Long stops are placed below entry and short stops above entry.

Close-based and frozen ATR is calculated through the actual detection candle and never moves afterward. The configured 1:R ratio and candle-8 time stop remain unchanged.
