01

Elliott Wave detection definitions

Default settings

Control how completed candles become swing pivots, how I–II–III–IV–V is validated, and which A–B–C corrections are accepted. One shared definition applies across all intervals.

How the values are measured Retracement (%) divides a corrective price distance by the actionary wave or complete impulse named in that option. Ratio (%) compares two wave lengths: 100% means equal length, 50% means half, and 200% means twice as long. ATR multiple (×) controls how far price must reverse before a new swing pivot exists.
About the “longest wave” rules

Wave III cannot be the shortest of I, III and V. You can also require Wave I to be shortest. That optional rule allows ties and overrides the setting that lets Wave I be longest.

Changes affect future alerts, newly calculated chart overlays, lower-degree hover counts, and newly reconstructed historical results. Already stored signals are not rewritten.
