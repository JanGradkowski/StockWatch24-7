01

Candlestick trend detection

Factory profile

Daily, Weekly, and Monthly detection uses two independent definitions of trend. The adaptive model verifies swing structure, regression direction, continuity, and terminal position. The original model separately verifies the configured percentage move and directional candle count. Either model can establish a trend unless they directly conflict.

Completed candles only The pattern candle or candles are excluded from the prior-trend window. The original move/count rule applies symmetrically to both uptrends and downtrends.
02

Daily detection

Adaptive trend structure plus an independently calculated original move/count rule.

Conflict-aware combined trend

A buy pattern is eligible when either model identifies a downtrend; a sell/short pattern is eligible when either identifies an uptrend. A sideways or unavailable result does not veto the other model, but opposite directional results reject the trend.

Active trend position

The last completed close before the pattern must still be meaningfully beyond the median close of the exact trend leg shown on the chart. The margin is volatility-adjusted rather than a hardcoded percentage.

Price movement

The first and last closes in the original rule's window must differ by at least this percentage.

Candle window

For example, 4 and 6 means at least four close or price-structure transitions must support the trend across the latest six completed candles.

Apply detection rules

Changes affect future scans and on-demand historical detection. Existing stored signals are not rewritten.
