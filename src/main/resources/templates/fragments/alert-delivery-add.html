Telegram

Get your alerts in a private chat with the StockWatch bot, on your phone and computer.

Open Telegram

Press Start in Telegram, then come back here. The link works for 15 minutes.

I pressed Start

Discord

In Discord: Server Settings, Integrations, Webhooks, New Webhook, then Copy Webhook URL.

Everyone who can read that Discord channel will see your alerts.

Webhook

StockWatch posts each alert as signed JSON to your own HTTPS address, for automation and your own tools.

Message format

Each alert is one POST with Standard Webhooks headers. Check the signature with your secret and reject timestamps older than five minutes; the same webhook-id on a retry is the same alert.
