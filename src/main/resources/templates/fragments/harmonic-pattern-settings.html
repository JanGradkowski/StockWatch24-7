Factory definitions

Control pivot construction, Fibonacci percentages, and the hard-versus-soft ratio gates used for Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab, Shark, and Cypher detection. One profile applies consistently to daily, weekly, and monthly formations.

Locked structural validity Direction, chronological pivot order, alternating highs/lows, inside/outside completion, and pattern-specific point boundaries always remain hard rules. A hard ratio accepts only the base Fibonacci tolerance. A soft ratio may exceed it by the pattern’s extra soft-rule allowance and loses score as it stretches. Changes affect future alerts and newly reconstructed overlays. Saved signals retain their original geometry and score evidence.
