Default settings

Set turning-point and Fibonacci rules for harmonic patterns. These settings apply to daily, weekly and monthly charts.

Required pattern rules Direction, chronological pivot order, alternating highs/lows, inside/outside completion, and pattern-specific point boundaries always remain hard rules. A hard ratio accepts only the base Fibonacci tolerance. A soft ratio may exceed it by the pattern’s extra soft-rule allowance and loses score as it stretches. Changes affect future alerts and newly reconstructed overlays. Saved signals retain their original geometry and score evidence.
