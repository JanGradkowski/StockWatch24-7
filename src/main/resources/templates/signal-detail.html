How the setup score works

A named candlestick pattern is emitted only after its mandatory geometry and prior-trend rules pass. V4 then allocates 25 points to pattern quality, 20 to EMA/SMA/MACD trend evidence, 5 to completed higher-timeframe trend, 15 to RSI/CCI momentum, 10 to Bollinger location, 15 to support/resistance, and 10 to relative volume, rolling VWAP, and an OHLCV volume-at-price approximation. Daily uses RSI 14, EMA 20/50, and SMA 200; weekly uses RSI 10, EMA 8/21, and SMA 40; monthly uses RSI 9, EMA 6/12, and SMA 24. The volume-profile values are approximations because an OHLCV candle does not reveal where inside its range each trade occurred. The score ranks heuristic confluence and cannot create or suppress an alert.

The Elliott detector combines wave structure, proportional relationships, trend context, momentum, and available confirmation evidence. Its score measures technical confluence, not probability of profit or financial advice.
