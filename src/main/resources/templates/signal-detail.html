How the setup score works

The pattern must meet its shape and prior-trend rules. Its score combines pattern quality, trend, momentum, Bollinger Bands, support/resistance and volume. Your scoring settings determine the weights. Volume-at-price estimates are approximate because candles do not show individual trades.

Required pattern rules must pass. The score then measures how closely the price ratios match the pattern. Your scoring settings determine the category weights.
