How portfolio performance is calculated

Portfolio value includes open demo buys and cash from closed buys. Each buy adds its investment; cash is not automatically reinvested. Demo sells are listed separately.

Return is profit or loss divided by starting capital plus new investments. Adding a trade is not a gain. Rankings combine buys of the same stock by invested value; closed positions only affect periods when they were held.

Prices are completed daily closes. Day compares the latest two closes; week looks back seven days and month one calendar month. Missing data is N/A. Currencies have separate totals. Fees, dividends, taxes and currency conversion are excluded.

Rankings show up to three stocks per list. With fewer than six stocks, a stock may appear in both lists.
