How it works

From watchlist to signal in three steps

  1. 1

    Build a watchlist

    Add the stocks, indexes and ETFs you follow, grouped into your own lists.

  2. 2

    StockWatch reads every close

    After each daily, weekly and monthly candle closes, it scores the technical outlook and looks for Elliott Waves, harmonic and candlestick patterns.

  3. 3

    You hear about what matters

    Write alerts from your own rules and get an email when one matches. Every signal is then followed to its outcome.
