Analysis, alerts and charting

Less chart watching.
More signal clarity.

Read an automated outlook, find Elliott Waves, harmonic and candlestick patterns, write alerts from your own rules and draw your analysis on the chart. Every signal is followed to its outcome, and demo trades test an idea without a real order.

01 / TECHNICAL OUTLOOK

Automated technical analysis

A buy, neutral or sell outlook scored from −100 to +100 on the daily, weekly and monthly interval. Seven indicator categories feed it, from trend and momentum to volume and recent pattern signals, and every score explains its inputs.

02 / CUSTOM ALERTS

Alerts from your own rules

Combine price, 104 indicators, candlestick, Elliott Wave and harmonic patterns, the outlook, and insider and congressional trades. A live preview replays the rule over the last 500 candles before you save it.

03 / ELLIOTT WAVES

Wave counts on the chart

Impulse waves 1–5 and A-B-C corrections on daily, weekly and monthly candles, labeled on the chart with Fibonacci targets and projections for the next wave.

04 / HARMONIC PATTERNS

11 harmonic patterns

Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab, Shark, Cypher, 5-0, AB=CD and their variants, each with labeled X-A-B-C-D points, ratios, a quality score and a structural stop.

05 / CANDLESTICK PATTERNS

17 candlestick patterns

From Hammer and Engulfing to Morning Star and Three White Soldiers, each checked for the trend before it and followed until it is confirmed, invalidated or expires.

06 / INDICATORS

121 chart indicators

Moving averages, trend, momentum, volatility, volume, levels, statistics and volume profiles, on the price chart or in their own panes, with indicator sets you can save.

07 / DRAWING

86 drawing tools

Trend lines, channels, pitchforks, Fibonacci and Gann tools, long and short positions, pattern and Elliott Wave tools, shapes and notes, saved to your account for each stock.

08 / OUTCOMES & DEMO TRADES

See what happened after the signal

Every signal carries an entry, stop and target and is followed to its result. Demo trades test an idea at the current quote without placing a real order.
